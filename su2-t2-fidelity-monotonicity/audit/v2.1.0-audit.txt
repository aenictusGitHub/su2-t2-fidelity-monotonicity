1. ALL-GROUP SPIN REPRESENTATION: ADDRESSED

SpinAction.lean proves, for every N and every fundamental SU(2) element U,
  tensorPower U N * embedding N = embedding N * action N U.
No invariant-range or intertwining premise is assumed. The proof first shows
that the row-generating polynomial of any 2x2 matrix's tensor power is
  (U00+U01 X)^(N-weight(b)) (U10+U11 X)^weight(b).
Coefficient equality implies constancy on row Hamming-weight classes. A
projection lemma proved from the normalized Dicke embedding then establishes
invariance of its range. Positivity of the binomial normalization is justified
from weight(b)<=N, including N=0.

The checked public results are:
  Dicke.action_intertwines
  Dicke.action_one
  Dicke.action_mul
  Dicke.action_unitary
  Dicke.unitaryAction

The last is a homomorphism from the actual SU(2) group into the unitary group
on Fin(N+1). Thus group composition and unitarity are explicitly certified,
in addition to the generator intertwiners used in the original theorem.

2. MAGNETIC-BASIS ORDER: ADDRESSED

BASIS_ORDER.md and the written proof specify the complete conversion between
Lean's m=k-N/2 coordinates and the manuscript numerical arrays' m=N/2-q order:
  q=N-k,
  v_asc=R v_desc,
  rho_asc=R rho_desc R,
  Phi_asc(A)=R Phi_desc(R A R) R,
where R is the reversal permutation, R=R^dagger=R^(-1). No additional phases
are part of this reordering. Both indices of an operator must be reversed.
The guide also states D_desc=D_asc R, identifying the same physical state.

BasisOrder.lean implements reverseVector, reverseMatrix, reverseChannel and
proves that vector and matrix reversal are involutions.
